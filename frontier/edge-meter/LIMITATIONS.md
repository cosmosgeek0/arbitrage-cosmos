# F8 EdgeMeter limitations

- Fixture mode only; no source adapter, transport, route, persistence, timer, credential, secret, or environment access exists.
- Owner references are synthetically supplied in fixtures. F8 validates their structure, exact revision/hash identity, owner class, time ordering, and a deterministic hash binding between each reference and its selected sibling projection. It has no production signature, trust root, canonical adapter, or owner readback, so it cannot prove the referenced payload until those adapters are supplied.
- Capital owns every economic measure, exact net, accounting policy, component inclusion decision, estimate, and unexplained residual. F8 copies the supplied projection without calculating a replacement total.
- Q owns partitions, holdout/embargo, point-in-time proof, calibration, abstention, uncertainty, multiplicity, deflated performance, and selection pressure. F8 displays supplied values without statistical recomputation.
- The gross timing bridge is explicitly non-accounting and is excluded from Capital net. Latency is not treated as money.
- Rejected, expired, missed, and control cohorts can carry reason/evidence projections only. Their monetary counterfactual is always `null`.
- Breakdowns group owner-supplied reference identities and statuses only; aggregate amounts and rankings are not produced.
- The preview is an inert, synthetic, unmounted local pre-adapter shaped exactly as the accepted Product surface projection contract; it has no controls or effects and is not a Product-owned canonical surface.
- CPU, memory, disk throughput, production latency, isolation, and production load behavior are unavailable in prestage. Only a deterministic fixture envelope is measured.
- Any future shared dependency or cockpit projection requires the director and each canonical owner to accept `INSERTION.md` first.
