# D0015 — explicit L07 consumption of L08.1 Spot capability

Status: `ACCEPTED_FOR_LANE_L08.2`

Date: `2026-07-13`

Authority: one additive, synthetic L07 owner-evidence event that reruns the
accepted L08.1/L04 Spot LIMIT/TIF binder. This decision grants no send, order,
ticket, strategy, Capital, Risk, execution, reconciliation, persistence,
transport or external-action authority.

## Decision

Add a distinct `SPOT_ORDER_TIF_PREFLIGHT_EVIDENCE` event rather than widening
the accepted `PREFLIGHT_EVIDENCE` event. Its closed payload binds one L07
fixture to the current L04 registry snapshot, complete sizing request and the
expected content-addressed L08.1 capability revision.

L07 derives the result itself by rerunning `bindSpotOrderTifCapability` and
`validateSpotOrderTifCapability`; no caller may supply a capability or a
positive preflight result. The retained event/state is replayed through the
same owner at venue arrival and checkpoint restore. A successful twin
preflight requires exact agreement with the expected capability revision and
the complete fixture, sizing, revision, time and non-send authority facts.

The new history variant is discriminated inside the existing `preflights`
array. The legacy four-field history entry, top-level twin state, order
fixture, checkpoint schema and fill mechanics remain unchanged. Legacy Spot
LIMIT evidence therefore remains fail-closed; Spot MARKET and USD-M retain
their existing L04 paths.

The admitted L08.1 surface is exactly Spot `LIMIT` with one of `GTC`, `IOC` or
`FOK`. `GTC` uses existing L07 resting/partial behavior, `IOC` expires the
unfilled remainder, and `FOK` is atomic. No `MARKET`, `LIMIT_MAKER`, conditional
order, GTD/GTX/PO TIF or send-capable operation is admitted by this seam.
