  no copied envelope enum/type authority.

  BOM rejection, bounded validation, recursive forbidden/unsafe content
  rejection, safe-integer enforcement, canonical-wire equality and detached
  deeply frozen accepted values.
- Compatibility is evaluated before semantic actionability. Unknown schema or
  version fails as `PROTOCOL_VERSION_UNSUPPORTED` without reflecting producer
  identities or raw/private text.
- Bounded assessment identity memory and FIFO sequence-sensitive queue with
  exact duplicate/conflict/revision rules, count/byte drop-new backpressure,
  no live-memory eviction, injected local receipt time and exact dwell expiry.

  higher revision is dropped, non-actionable, assessment-expired or
  economic-expired.

  causal/future time order, source quality, evidence completeness, OOD/drift,
  economics, fees/funding/borrow, spread/slippage, latency/recovery, regime and
  registered strategy family.

  last-success order/freshness, truth label, model lineage, drift and blockers
  gate advisory dispatch. Foreign or conflicting remote health fails closed.
- The only strategy seam is an injected immutable advisory observation port.
  It returns a bounded owner reference or named rejection and has no proposal,
  Capital, Risk, control, ticket, execution, exchange, persistence or order
  method.
- Recording health is a read-only injected canonical projection. Recording
  degradation lowers research-recording truth but does not gate an otherwise
  healthy advisory observation and creates no recovery dependency.
- Outcome/feedback projection validates frozen schemas and direction, rejects
  account/private content, preserves `REAL` versus `HYPOTHETICAL`, and binds

  producer-declared hash.
- Local transport policy accepts only exact plain-data literal loopback HTTP or
