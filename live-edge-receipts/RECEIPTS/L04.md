## Delivered behavior

- The adapter consumes only injected, immutable, exact-owner
  `RuntimeListingMetadata`. It creates one bounded value snapshot and performs
  no fetch, socket, timer, filesystem, environment, cache, store or refresh.
- Runtime source IDs, datasets, source-record identity, transform provenance,
  product and L02 contract identity are exact. Host/response-hash evidence
  absent from Runtime metadata remains a mandatory recorded loss rather than
  being inferred.
- Symbol, base and quote tokens must already be canonical uppercase NFC values
  in the closed Unicode letter/number/dot/underscore/hyphen grammar. Attacker
  whitespace, case and decomposed text are rejected, not normalized into a
  trusted owner identity.
- Inputs are bounded to 10,000 symbols, 128 filters per symbol and independent
  graph, key and aggregate-string budgets. Accessors, proxies, inherited
  values, sparse arrays, unsafe text, unknown fields and mutation attempts
  fail closed without truncation.
- One canonical filter-schema owner validates the exact current Spot/Margin
  and USD-M shapes. Raw and canonical values, disabled semantics,
  applicability, capability losses and source content digests remain
  immutable and explicit.
- New-order posture requires exact `TRADING`. Unknown, stale, missing,
  unsupported, disabled, contradictory, duplicate or product-cross-cast truth
  is non-actionable. Margin always remains blocked pending its separate owner
  joins.
- Exact decimals reuse only Capital Foundation's approved decimal leaf. No
  binary floating-point money/filter arithmetic, exponent notation, locale
  formatting, UI precision or legacy exchange rounding is used.
- Price is never adjusted. Quantity may stay unchanged or reduce by an exact
  downward grid operation; incompatible grids block and no algorithm searches
  upward. Exact notional is recomputed after every transformation.
- Tick, step, min/max quantity, market quantity, min/max notional,
  applicability, status, order type, time in force and percent-price rules are
  checked at exact boundaries. A positive static decision remains
  non-sendable and carries `externalActionAuthorization=NONE` and
  `actionAuthority=NONE_RECORD_ONLY`.
- The effective notional ceiling is the minimum of Capital-approved notional,
  intended exposure, applicable official maximum and the unchanged 10-USDT
  canary. Literal `10` is applied only when both instrument quote asset and
  request notional asset are exact `USDT`; non-USDT blocks because L04 owns no
  FX conversion. Minimum-filter conflict never increases quantity or capital.
- Percent-price and market-notional decisions require a closed, current,
  Runtime-owned reference-evidence object bound to product, symbol,
  instrument revision, reference kind/window and exact decimal price. Its
  injected validity interval cannot exceed 5,000 ms.
- Semantic `instrumentRevision` and `registryRevision` are derived from
  canonical product/environment/source/filter content and remain stable across
  receipt-only refreshes. Separate `sourceEvidenceRevision` and
  `snapshotRevision` bind source times, receipt, evaluation and evidence
  instance so timestamp mutation cannot manufacture freshness.
- Every sizing result echoes semantic, evidence, snapshot and reference
  revisions plus temporal bounds. No caller may substitute one revision layer
  for another.
- The pure waiting decision binds symbol, semantic/evidence/snapshot revisions,
  injected evaluation time, exact order type, time in force, USDT notional
  asset and the complete reference-evidence object. Revision, posture, content
  or expiry changes require re-preflight; all outcomes retain
  `sendEligible=false`.
- No acquisition service, registry singleton, cache, proposal queue, Capital
  or Risk verdict, order constructor, execution/reconciliation event or
  persistence authority was created.
