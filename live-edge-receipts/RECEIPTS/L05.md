## Explicit state-commitment limitation

The Spot/USD-M state commitments and related content revisions are
deterministic unkeyed SHA-256 comparison identifiers. They prove that supplied
commitments match the canonical content of the supplied state; they are not
keyed signatures, Binance/source attestations, authentication, durable history
or externally persisted previous-state anchors.

Partial erasure, stale commitment, retained-marker/cut retag, accounting
deletion, incoherent continuity rewrite and quality rewind with retained
evidence fail closed or remain witness-blocking. A caller that replaces the
entire supplied state with another independently coherent history can generate
a different valid commitment. A deterministic evidence-only snapshot validator
cannot disprove that alternate history without a trusted prior anchor. Only the
existing Ledger/System Integration owners may anchor lineage; L05 creates no
parallel history owner and makes no provenance claim.
