## Surviving implementation

- One bounded append-only value journal binds the existing correlation,
  Strategy, Capital, Foundation plan/leg, L03 request/client-order, product,
  account realm, contract, L04 preflight and L05 private-truth identities.
- `prepare*` functions construct immutable append requests; exact injected
  acknowledgements validate expected version, prior/next heads, entry content,
  request digest, owner, Ledger event identity and commit time. Failed,
  malformed, stale or partial acknowledgements cannot produce persisted truth.
- The synthetic submission boundary requires a committed
  `SUBMISSION_INTENT`, remains explicitly non-sendable and can append the exact
  synthetic attempt observation only once under compare-and-append semantics.
- Per-kind and total bounds reserve the final eight slots for money-critical
  recovery evidence. Telemetry is coalesced and cannot consume those slots.
- Timeout, disconnect, 408, 5xx and non-408 4xx evidence stays `UNKNOWN` until
  an exact current Foundation reconciliation observation clears it. Private
  working/fill/terminal axes remain visible without overwriting ambiguity.
- Compatible HTTP/private arrival orders converge to one semantic outcome.
  Later exchange-time fills after `CANCELED`, `EXPIRED` or `REJECTED`, terminal
  regressions, overfills, cumulative regressions, identity forks and private/
  owner terminal disagreements become named conflicts.
- Foundation owner input is bounded and secret-safe. A full-result content
  commitment is retained separately from an independently reproducible closed
  L06 projection commitment. Quantity, status, remainder and owner-component
  consistency are revalidated; callers cannot author the effect.
- Foundation binary64 comparison is restricted to the exact safe-integer
  intersection. Fractional comparison remains `UNKNOWN` pending I00.
- Query/reconciliation hand-off contains deterministic identity-only evidence
  and grants no query, retry or reconciliation authority.
- Section 7 keeps the 10-USDT ceiling fixed, emits only owner-review evidence,
  preserves clock domains, records no empirical region benchmark and fails
  closed when a historical durable-intent ACK timestamp cannot be recovered.
