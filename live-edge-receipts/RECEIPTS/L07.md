## Surviving implementation

- One closed deterministic reducer accepts only explicit synthetic fixtures,
  injected timestamps, event/source sequences, exact-decimal book evidence,
  model revision and seed. Every value remains deeply frozen, bounded and
  marked `SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE`, `NONE_RECORD_ONLY` and `NONE`.
- Exact canonical event payloads are retained. Every checkpoint, restore and
  incremental reduction reproduces the full state from those events, so a
  recomputed self-hash cannot bless forged order, fill, book, fee, owner or
  local-observation state.
- L03 rate decisions, L04 preflight outcomes and L06 placement/cancel
  identities are retained and re-evaluated through their owner seams. L07
  creates no second limiter, instrument registry, mutation journal or
  reconciliation owner.
- Books reject crossed, unsorted, duplicate, replayed, regressed, gapped and
  overbound evidence. Aggressive execution consumes only eligible explicit
  opposite-side depth; passive execution requires explicit trade-through and
  an injected estimated queue position.
- Canonical ordering uses occurrence time, optional source sequence with
  absent values ordered last, conservative priority and stable sequence/ID.
  Conflicting equal-time events without two distinct source sequences remain
  `AMBIGUOUS`; ambiguous book truth is gapped and never falsely fresh.
- Same-price passive orders use proved arrival source order and non-overlapping
  absolute queue positions. Shared trade quantity is consumed once per price
  group; overlapping positions enter recovery posture rather than minting a
  fill. Trade-through depletes better book levels before the reported price.
- `GTC`, `IOC`, `FOK` and `MARKET` behavior stays exact and conservative.
  Requested quantity, ACK and quote removal are never fill evidence. The
  aggregate fill boundary accepts exactly 1,024 fills and rejects the 1,025th
  during reduction.
- Venue-oracle and local observation remain distinct. HTTP ambiguity, stale
  private delivery, crash/restart and cancel-delivery ambiguity cannot promote
  a venue effect into local truth. A cancel ACK observes only a proven
  `CANCELED` effect or preserves a stronger prior exact observation.
- Exact fee evidence is content-addressed and retained. Maker/taker units,
  charge basis, fee asset and optional conversion pair/unit are reproduced per
  fill. Missing quote conversion and missing cost observations remain `null`,
  never fabricated zero.
- Calibration compares versioned predicted and recorded evidence only. It
  validates product, symbol, quantity asset, model/sample/freshness lineage and
  exact residuals; it does not train, fit, persist or promote a model.
