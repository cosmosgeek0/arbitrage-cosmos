## Compatibility and bounds laws

- Raw UTF-8 frame is at most 262,144 bytes before parsing; malformed input, invalid UTF-8, noncanonical wire bytes, duplicate keys, excessive depth/nodes/issues, unsafe integers and nonfinite values fail closed.
- Compatibility is evaluated before actionability. Unknown schema or version other than 1 returns `PROTOCOL_VERSION_UNSUPPORTED`, even if the same frame is expired.
- Schema validation uses own-property checks. Inherited names such as `constructor`, `__proto__` and `toString` cannot bypass `additionalProperties: false`.
- Designated open schema maps remain open as the frozen schema specifies; forbidden-key scanning still recurses through them.
- All retained input is detached, canonicalized and deeply frozen.
- No raw payload or private sentinel is echoed into failure, snapshot, health or receipt output.

## Assessment actionability laws

- Preserve event, peer receive, generation, expiry and L-local receipt times separately.
- Effective expiry is `min(expiryTimeMs, economicAssumptionContext.expiresAtMs)`; `nowMs >= effectiveExpiryMs` is expired, at ingest and immediately before dispatch.
- `STALE`/`UNKNOWN` source quality, OOD/unknown OOD, drifting/broken/unknown drift, unknown economics, missing required economics, named conflicting evidence, clock skew, `ABSTAIN_UNKNOWN`, unsupported family or incompatible revision is non-actionable.
- `regimeProbabilities` remains versioned advisory evidence. Missing/empty/unsupported/stale/conflicting regime is `UNKNOWN`; L01 computes no cap and creates no regime supervisor.
- `PROPOSE_CONSIDERATION` may call exactly one registered advisory owner port after all gates. That port cannot create proposals, approvals, reservations, risk verdicts, tickets, controls or orders.
- Other advisory actions are observed/rejected only; no money or control authority is created.

## Identity/backpressure laws

- Assessment identity key is `(assessmentId, revision)` plus an L-policy SHA-256 fingerprint of canonical bytes.
- Identical redelivery is `DUPLICATE_NOOP`; same key/different fingerprint poisons the identity as `ASSESSMENT_IDENTITY_CONFLICT`; lower revision is `ASSESSMENT_REVISION_STALE`.
- A higher revision may advance without requiring contiguity but cannot change configured immutable route identity.
- Identity memory is bounded. Unexpired records are never evicted to admit new work; capacity exhaustion fails closed.
- Queue is FIFO and sequence-sensitive. It never coalesces or evicts a prior assessment. Count/byte overflow drops the new item and records degradation.
- Queue uses injected L-local receipt time, never peer-controlled receive time. Exact dwell deadline times out.
- No timer, background loop, socket, filesystem, environment read, wall clock or autonomous retry exists in pure components.

## Health and Section 7 laws

- Startup is `UNKNOWN` and not ready.
- Local readiness combines local transport evidence, compatibility, heartbeat age, remote state/blockers/drift/lineage and queue pressure.
- Heartbeat age equal to TTL is stale. Remote degradation/failure/unknown is never upgraded. `truthLabel: LIVE` does not imply `LIVE_PUBLIC` or money authority.
- Canonical Intelligence Runtime recording health is consumed through a read-only injected port/projection only. Recording failure degrades research truth but cannot block engine observation, active-position supervision, reconciliation or recovery.
- Q unavailability never stops deterministic existing engines or money-critical recovery paths.
