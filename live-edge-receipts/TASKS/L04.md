# L04 — instrument and filter registry

Status: `COMPLETE`

## Purpose

Build a deterministic, no-I/O normalization and validation seam for current
Binance Spot, USD-M and Margin instrument/filter truth. Consume injected
official exchange-info evidence, preserve exact decimal strings, issue a
content-bound immutable revision and prove that no rounding or filter path can
silently increase quantity, approved notional or intended exposure.

This phase does not acquire exchange data, own a cache/store, read credentials
or accounts, sign or send a request, open a socket, submit a test/live order,
mutate Margin, deploy, restart or change capital. Existing Intelligence Runtime
remains public-source acquisition/recording owner; L04 is a narrow normalizer
and preflight adapter only.

## Required implementation

### Injected exchange-info normalization

- Accept only closed, bounded, plain-data payloads whose product/environment
  identity is bound to an exact validated L02 current exchange-info contract.
- Normalize each relevant current symbol into an immutable instrument spec with
  product, symbol, assets, status, exact price/quantity/notional/market limits,
  contract size where applicable, allowed order types/time-in-force, injected
  source time, content hash and revision identity.
- Include every relevant current filter proved by official documentation and
  captured fixtures; the directive example is not exhaustive.
- Unknown, absent, disabled, contradictory, duplicate, malformed or unsupported
  filters remain explicit and non-actionable. Never fabricate zero, infinity,
  default precision, market applicability or trading readiness.
- Preserve product differences. Spot, USD-M and Margin status/filter semantics
  cannot be cross-cast or inferred from a sibling product.
- Perform no fetch, socket, filesystem, environment, timer or ambient clock
  access. Acquisition and refresh are injected owner responsibilities.

### Exact decimal and sizing law

- Parse and compare decimal strings without binary floating-point conversion,
  exponent notation, locale formatting or UI-derived precision.
- Separate price alignment from quantity reduction. Name every transformation
  and preserve requested versus resulting values.
- Never round quantity upward. If a contract requires any upward adjustment,
  return a named block unless Capital and every limit are revalidated by their
  canonical owners in a later phase.
- Recalculate exact notional after every price or quantity transformation.
- Enforce tick size, step size, min/max quantity, market quantity, min/max
  notional, applicability flags, status and allowed order-type/time-in-force
  constraints at exact boundaries.
- A successful filter result may reduce size but cannot exceed requested
  quantity, approved notional, intended exposure or any supplied hard ceiling.

### Revision and waiting-proposal safety

- Derive a deterministic revision from canonical normalized content plus bound
  product/environment/source identity; injected receipt time alone cannot
  rewrite semantic identity.
- Reject duplicate symbol identity with conflicting content and reject source
  hash/revision mismatch.
- Bind every validation result to the exact instrument revision it consumed.
- Provide a pure re-preflight decision that invalidates or blocks a waiting
  proposal when symbol status or any relevant filter revision changes. Never
  permit send eligibility from stale filter evidence.
- This is documentary/preflight state only: no proposal queue, durable store,
  Capital/Risk verdict, execution plan or order authority is created.

## Acceptance

- Current official exchange-info/filter fixtures normalize into exact,
  revision-bound immutable specs through an injected, network-free seam.
- No rounding/filter path exceeds approved notional, intended exposure or
  requested quantity, and no path silently raises size.
- Status/filter changes invalidate or require re-preflight of waiting work;
  stale revisions cannot remain eligible.
- Product/filter unknowns and conflicts remain explicit and blocking.
- No duplicate acquisition, cache, proposal queue, strategy, Capital, Risk,
  execution, reconciliation, ledger or runtime authority is created.
- Focused L-lane, frozen protocol, standard and full accumulated suites pass.
- Adversarial corrections, implementation commit, immutable receipt and state
  transition are complete before L05 begins.

## Section 7 and external boundary

- The unchanged 10-USDT canary envelope is a hard external ceiling, not an L04
  sizing target. The filter seam may reduce or block but cannot automatically
  increase capital or size to satisfy minimum filters.
- A minimum-notional conflict with the canary envelope is explicit
  `FILTER_BLOCKED`/unavailable truth; it never triggers a capital increase.
- Region/event-to-private-confirmation latency remains owner-gated and is not
  measured or fabricated by filter normalization.
- Regime posture remains advisory through existing Strategy/Capital/Risk; L04
  cannot turn a quant assessment into an order.
- Recording remains through canonical Intelligence Runtime; the registry does
  not create a second feed/cache or make recording depend on execution.
- No private account read, signed/test/live order, AWS/Cloudflare/Secrets
  mutation, deployment/restart, Margin action, real order, capital change or
  automated live entry is authorized by this task.
