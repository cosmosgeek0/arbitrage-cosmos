# L05 — private truth and separate streams

Status: `COMPLETE`

## Purpose

Build or harden a deterministic, no-I/O private-evidence boundary for Spot,
USD-M and Margin that preserves each product's independent freshness,
permissions, modes, commissions, stream lifecycle and reconciliation posture.
Incomplete, stale, mismatched or unsupported truth must block entry without
inventing account readiness.

This bounded phase may implement pure parsers, immutable projections, lifecycle
reducers and canonical-owner hand-off adapters using only synthetic/injected
fixtures. It does not read a private account, credentials or secrets; acquire a
listen key; sign or send a request; open a socket; submit a test/live order;
borrow, repay or mutate Margin; deploy, restart or change capital.

## Required implementation

### Canonical-owner private truth projection

- Consume only closed, bounded, immutable, synthetic/injected evidence. Every
  fact carries exact product, account/environment realm, source contract,
  schema, receipt/source time and revision identity.
- Preserve distinct `permissions`, `spot`, `futures`, `margin`, `modes`,
  `commissions`, per-source freshness, completeness and reconciliation
  mismatch truth. A fresh product or source cannot promote a stale/unknown
  sibling.
- Use exact decimal strings for balances, positions, liabilities, fees,
  interest and rates. Unknown is never zero; missing is never empty; HTTP
  success is never fill or reconciliation truth.
- Generate only sanitized, immutable canonical-owner hand-off facts. Do not
  create a second account store, Capital budget, Risk check, reconciliation
  algorithm, ledger, proposal queue, execution state or supervisor.
- Bound symbols, assets, orders, trades, positions, events, strings, keys,
  graph depth and revision material. Reject accessors, inherited values,
  proxies, sparse arrays, duplicate/conflicting identities and unknown fields.

### Required product facts

- Spot: exact free/locked balances, open orders, recent trades/fills,
  commissions where available and account/permission/status posture.
- USD-M: wallet/available balance, positions, entry/mark/unrealized PnL,
  position mode, single/multi-asset mode, relevant leverage/margin type, open
  orders/trades, income/funding/fees and liquidation/ADL posture where proved.
- Margin: account mode, collateral/free/locked, borrowed/interest/net asset,
  maximum-borrowable/inventory/rate evidence, open orders/trades, liabilities
  and repayment state.
- Facts not present in an exact current contract remain `UNKNOWN`,
  `UNAVAILABLE`, `PARTIAL` or a named mismatch. Never infer Margin eligibility
  from Spot permission or a public symbol flag.

### Permissions, modes and secret boundary

- Model read and execution permission profiles as separate identities. A
  combined profile is unsupported without an explicit later owner exception.
- Preserve exact Spot/Margin trade, Futures, read/account, withdrawal,
  transfer, fixed-IP and account-fingerprint/realm evidence. Unknown or
  policy-conflicting posture blocks; withdrawal/transfer capability is never
  added.
- Unsupported or mismatched portfolio Margin, multi-assets, hedge mode,
  isolated/cross Margin or account realm remains explicit and blocking.
- Never accept, retain, log, hash into public diagnostics or project a secret,
  API key value, private key, signature, listen key or credential material.

### Separate private-stream lifecycle reducers

- Keep Spot, USD-M and Margin lifecycle state and freshness independent. A
  generic connected flag or public-stream health cannot establish private
  readiness.
- Spot follows the exact selected current WebSocket API private subscription,
  JSON encoding, authentication/session, subscription, unsubscribe,
  termination and lifetime posture from L02. A legacy Spot listen-key route
  must fail closed.
- USD-M follows its exact selected listen-key REST lifecycle and routed private
  raw/combined WebSocket contracts. Planned reconnect precedes documented
  expiry; keepalive/close identity and weights cannot be cross-cast.
- Margin risk-stream lifecycle remains separate from account/trading truth and
  uses only exact non-conflicting L02 contracts. The recorded close-parameter
  conflict remains blocked rather than guessed.
- Reducers track connected, authenticated, subscribed, connection generation,
  route class, exchange/local event times, planned expiry/reconnect,
  reconnect/duplicate/malformed counts, last order/fill/account event and last
  successful REST reconciliation.
- Clocks, retry/backoff draws, lifecycle observations and reconciliation cuts
  are injected. Implement no socket, timer, background loop, credential call,
  request builder or transport scheduler.
- Event deduplication and ordering are deterministic and bounded. Do not claim
  a global gap-free sequence when the product contract does not provide one.

### Freshness, completeness and reconciliation hand-off

- Evaluate freshness independently per fact source with injected time,
  explicit source-versus-receipt authority, exact expiry boundaries and
  bounded future skew. A stream being connected is insufficient without fresh
  REST reconciliation.
- Streams are timely evidence; signed REST is reconciliation. L05 may produce
  an immutable comparison input and named mismatch vocabulary for the existing
  Foundation reconciliation owner, but it may not decide canonical
  reconciliation or clear an ambiguous mutation.
- Overall completeness is `COMPLETE` only when every fact required by the exact
  requested product/action posture is fresh, coherent and reconciled. Partial,
  stale, source-error, rate-limited, unsupported, realm-mismatched or
  permission-missing evidence blocks entry.
- Preserve external/manual orders, positions and liabilities as foreign truth;
  never authorize broad cancellation or hide them from the canonical owners.
- Bind each projection and lifecycle decision to its exact source and content
  revisions so a waiting L04 preflight cannot substitute stale private truth.

## Acceptance

- Exact injected Spot, USD-M and Margin evidence produces immutable,
  revision-bound private truth with independent product/source freshness.
- Incomplete, stale, mismatched, unsupported, permission-conflicting or
  unreconciled truth deterministically blocks entry and cannot be upgraded by a
  connected stream or fresh sibling product.
- Separate current product lifecycle reducers preserve exact route/auth/expiry
  differences and reject obsolete or conflicting route assumptions.
- Existing Capital, Foundation reconciliation/risk/borrow, Ledger and System
  Integration owners remain canonical; no duplicate private runtime/store or
  authority is created.
- Focused L-lane, frozen protocol, standard and full accumulated suites pass.
- Adversarial corrections, implementation commit, immutable receipt and state
  transition are complete before L06 begins.

## Section 7 and external boundary

- The unchanged 10-USDT canary envelope remains a hard ceiling. L05 truth may
  block or support later evidence review but cannot increase capital,
  leverage, liability or any envelope automatically.
- Build an injected event-to-private-confirmation latency evidence seam only if
  it composes with existing owner truth. No empirical region/account benchmark
  is claimed without separately authorized runtime observation.
- Regime posture remains advisory through existing Strategy/Capital/Risk;
  private truth cannot turn a quant assessment into an order.
- Recording remains through canonical Intelligence Runtime and must remain
  independent of execution, reconciliation and recovery availability.
- No private account read, credential access, signed/test/live order,
  AWS/Cloudflare/Secrets mutation, deployment/restart, Margin action, real
  order, capital change or automated live entry is authorized by this task.
