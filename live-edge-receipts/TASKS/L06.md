# L06 — durable mutation journal and reducer

Status: `COMPLETE`

## Purpose

Build a deterministic, no-I/O journal protocol and order-mutation reducer that
adapts to the existing canonical Ledger transaction boundary. Prove
persist-before-send, identity lineage, append-only transitions, event/HTTP
race convergence, idempotency and ambiguity hand-off without creating a second
database, outbox service, ticket owner, exchange client or execution authority.

This phase operates only on injected records, persistence acknowledgements and
synthetic observations. It does not persist to the production database, call a
private account, sign or send a request, submit a test/live order, acquire a
listen key, open a socket, mutate Margin/capital/cloud state, deploy or restart.

## Required implementation

### Canonical-ledger journal adapter contract

- Define a closed, bounded, immutable append protocol for the existing Ledger
  transaction boundary. The adapter may construct journal entries, batches and
  expected-version compare-and-append requests; it may not write files/SQLite,
  own a connection, migrate schema, allocate a singleton or claim persistence.
- Require an exact durable append acknowledgement before a mutation may move
  from prepared intent to the synthetic send boundary. Missing, rejected,
  conflicting, stale-version, partial or malformed acknowledgement blocks send.
- Express atomic persist-before-send and checkpoint/outbox requirements in a
  narrow director-owned integration patch/request if existing Ledger APIs do
  not expose them safely. Never report the patch as wired.
- Keep the mutation journal money-critical and independent from noncritical
  read-model/telemetry persistence. Telemetry failure cannot erase journal
  truth or remove recovery posture.

### Identity graph and closed records

- Bind global correlation, assessment/decision, strategy/proposal, Capital
  mandate/reservation, ticket, leg, deterministic client order, optional
  Binance order, trade/fill and reconciliation identities.
- Bind product, environment, account realm, exact L02 contract identity,
  operation/mutation kind, L04 instrument/filter/preflight revisions, L05
  private source/lifecycle/reconciliation revisions, exchange/local times and
  sanitized request/response content hashes.
- Treat absent exchange IDs as explicit unknown. Reject aliases, duplicates,
  conflicting edges, cross-ticket/client-order reuse, realm/product swaps,
  unsafe text, secret-shaped fields, unknown fields and unbounded graphs.
- Reuse existing Foundation/Capital/ticket identities as references only; do
  not mint strategy, mandate, risk, reconciliation or mechanical-plan verdicts.

### Append-only mutation state

- Model the closed progression `CREATED -> PREFLIGHTED -> SUBMITTING ->
  ACKNOWLEDGED|UNKNOWN|REJECTED -> NEW -> PARTIALLY_FILLED ->
  FILLED|CANCELED|EXPIRED -> RECONCILED`, with explicit non-effects,
  impossibility and manual/recovery posture.
- `SUBMITTING` requires persisted intent. A synthetic submission observation
  can be accepted only once for the exact persisted mutation identity.
- Preserve `UNKNOWN` and `PARTIAL_MIXED`; never map unknown to not-sent or an
  error string to retryable. Mutation retry/replacement requires exact
  operation-specific idempotency plus canonical reconciliation evidence.
- Events may precede HTTP acknowledgement. Exact duplicates are idempotent;
  out-of-order compatible observations converge; contradictions remain named
  conflicts; impossible transitions fail closed without deleting prior truth.
- Track requested, acknowledged, working, partially filled, terminal and
  reconciled truth separately. Acknowledgement is not fill; HTTP success is
  not exchange effect; cancel/replace or batch outcomes may remain mixed.
- All counters, histories, identity edges, texts, arrays, hashes and revisions
  are bounded. Reducers use injected time and are deterministic and deeply
  immutable.

### Ambiguity and reconciliation hand-off

- Consume the accepted L03 ambiguity vocabulary and L05 canonical owner cuts.
  Timeout, disconnect, 5xx or persistence degradation after a possible send
  becomes durable recovery-required evidence keyed by deterministic client
  order ID; it never authorizes blind recreation.
- Build only sanitized immutable query/reconciliation input for existing
  Foundation/System Integration owners. L06 cannot query Binance, decide
  exchange truth, clear ambiguity or create a reconciliation result.
- Reconciliation may advance to `RECONCILED` only from an exact injected owner
  result bound to the journal revision, mutation identity, product/account
  realm, client/exchange IDs and complete observed effect. Unknown, partial,
  stale, mismatched or foreign/manual truth remains visible and blocking.
- Preserve actual fills, external/manual orders and unknown effects. Never
  erase exposure, liabilities or recovery merely because local telemetry or a
  response is absent.

### Capital–evidence and latency seams

- The unchanged 10-USDT canary remains a hard ceiling. Emit at most a versioned
  evidence candidate for later owner-approved promotion/demotion; it cannot
  raise capital and Capital/Risk remain sole authorities.
- Bind injected latency timestamps from approval through durable intent,
  synthetic submission, acknowledgement and private confirmation where
  available. Unknown segments stay unknown. Do not claim an empirical region
  benchmark or call an exchange/account.
- Regime posture remains an advisory revision through existing owners; journal
  state cannot turn a model assessment into an order.
- Canonical Intelligence Runtime recording health remains separately visible;
  recording degradation cannot block money-critical reconciliation/recovery or
  create a duplicate feed/journal.

## Deterministic tests

- Persist failure, malformed/partial/stale/conflicting append acknowledgement
  and compare-version conflict all block the synthetic send boundary.
- Persisted intent permits exactly one matching synthetic submission
  observation; duplicate replay never creates a second send authorization.
- HTTP-before-event, event-before-HTTP, duplicate, out-of-order, partial-fill,
  terminal-before-ack, cancel/fill race, timeout/disconnect/5xx and
  crash/recovery fixtures converge conservatively.
- `UNKNOWN` cannot become not-sent/rejected/filled without exact owner
  reconciliation. Query-by-client-order hand-off is deterministic and carries
  no request or retry authority.
- Exact identity graph, cross-product/account/ticket conflicts, missing/unknown
  exchange IDs, secret/unsafe/hostile graphs, bounds and revision substitution
  fail closed.
- Acknowledged is not working/fill, requested quantity is not actual fill,
  terminal state is not reconciled, and partial/mixed effects remain visible.
- Same records in compatible orders yield the same canonical state/outcome
  revision; contradictory histories yield named conflict, never false green.
- Crash snapshots at every append state reconstruct from the same accepted
  journal records without duplicate synthetic submission or erased ambiguity.
- Telemetry/recording failure does not erase the durable-journal candidate or
  block recovery hand-off.
- Proof that no result grants persistence, signer, transport, send, retry,
  reconciliation, Capital, Risk, ticket, Margin or external-action authority.
- Import/static purity: no network, fs, env, SQLite, timer, randomness,
  credential, existing exchange client, duplicate ledger/store or money path.

## Acceptance

- Persist-before-send is an exact durable acknowledgement gate and a failed or
  ambiguous persistence path cannot reach the synthetic submission boundary.
- Duplicate, out-of-order and event-before-response fixtures converge without
  duplicate send, false fill, erased ambiguity or hidden external truth.
- The journal/reducer is append-only, bounded, deterministic, reconstructable
  and identity/revision/account-realm bound.
- Existing Ledger, Foundation execution/reconciliation, Capital and System
  Integration owners remain canonical; L06 creates no database, journal
  service, execution/reconciliation verdict or production wiring.
- Focused L-lane, frozen protocol, standard and full accumulated suites pass.
- Adversarial corrections, implementation commit, immutable receipt and state
  transition are complete before L07 begins.

## Hard external boundary

No private/account read, credential access, signed/test/live order, real send,
query, listen key, socket, Margin action, capital change, AWS/Cloudflare/
Secrets mutation, deployment, restart or push is authorized. All submission,
HTTP, private-event, persistence and reconciliation observations are injected
synthetic fixtures. `LIVE_EXECUTION_NOT_IMPLEMENTED` remains enforced.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- No second SQLite/file store, ledger, outbox daemon, ticket store, proposal
  queue, execution coordinator, reconciliation engine or identity authority.
- No send-before-persist, blind retry, response-success-as-fill, unknown-as-no-
  effect, requested-as-filled, state deletion, history rewrite or silent alias.
- No credential material, signed payload, listen-key value, endpoint client,
  private reader, socket, timer, scheduler or background loop.
- No shared/director-owned edit except an inert narrow patch/request under
  `live-edge-receipts/patches/`; no frozen-protocol change or L07 work.
