# L07 — deterministic exchange twin

Status: `READY`

## Purpose

Build a pure, deterministic, fault-injection exchange twin that proves
execution state, conservative fill mechanics, observable-versus-venue truth
and recovery behavior for injected synthetic scenarios. It is a test and
policy-evidence adapter, not a paper-profit engine, exchange client, order
authority, persistence owner or production trading process.

Acceptance requires identical inputs/events to produce identical state and
outcome hashes, and conservative mode must never fill beyond requested
quantity or unconsumed explicit book liquidity.

## Authority and integration boundary

- The twin consumes injected closed fixtures and canonical owner references.
  It never authorizes, signs, constructs for transport, sends, queries,
  cancels or retries an exchange action.
- L06 does not retain side, order type, time in force or canonical submitted
  terms. L07 must accept an explicit closed `TwinOrderFixture` bound to L04/L06
  revisions and label it `SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE`; it must not pretend those terms
  were recovered from L06.
- L03 remains rate/ambiguity owner and L04 remains static filter/sizing owner.
  Reuse their pure seams rather than creating a second limiter or validator.
- Foundation remains execution and reconciliation owner. The twin may model a
  venue oracle and local observation but may not emit a canonical fill,
  position, reconciliation verdict, ticket transition or supervisor truth.
- Every artifact is deeply frozen, bounded and carries
  `simulationAuthority=SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE`,
  `actionAuthority=NONE_RECORD_ONLY` and
  `externalActionAuthorization=NONE`.
- Production binding remains director-owned and later-phase work.

## Required implementation

### Closed deterministic event model

- Use exact injected timestamps, sequence/event IDs, model revision and seed.
  No ambient clock, timers, sockets, network, filesystem, environment or
  `Math.random()`.
- Define closed event envelopes with schema, ID, sequence, occurrence time,
  kind and payload. Reject duplicate sequence, same-ID content conflicts,
  unknown fields, hostile graphs, unsafe values and resource overflow.
- Order events deterministically by occurrence time, conservative event
  priority and stable sequence/ID. Same inputs in any accepted insertion order
  must produce one canonical scenario and outcome hash.
- Keep venue-oracle state separate from locally observable state. A timeout,
  5xx, disconnect or crash may leave local state `UNKNOWN` while an injected
  venue event proves a fill in the oracle plane. Oracle truth is never exposed
  as reconciled cockpit truth.
- Bound events, orders, book levels, fills, scenarios, fees, strings and
  serialized snapshots. Checkpoint/restore must preserve hashes and prevent
  duplicate processing.

### Book and liquidity model

- Consume explicit versioned L2 snapshots/deltas with exact decimal strings,
  product, symbol, source sequence and validity.
- Maintain side-correct sorted price levels and per-book-revision consumed
  liquidity. A displayed quantity cannot be reused until a fresh source
  revision explicitly replenishes it.
- Aggressive fills occur only against opposite-side liquidity present at venue
  arrival, never beyond the submitted limit, remaining order quantity or
  unconsumed explicit depth. Preserve per-level fills and exact VWAP.
- Reject crossed, unsorted, duplicate, regressed, gapped or overbound book
  evidence unless the scenario explicitly models a named unavailable/gapped
  state.
- Public L2 cannot prove exact queue position. Passive queue state is
  `ESTIMATED` or `UNKNOWN`, never exact.

### Order, TIF and conservative fill law

- Support only the L04-proved static request subset: `LIMIT` and `MARKET`
  fixtures with explicit `GTC`, `IOC` or `FOK` where applicable. Do not widen
  L04 with `LIMIT_MAKER`, post-only, `GTX`, price-match or STP behavior.
- Re-run the current injected L04 static preflight at venue arrival. The twin
  accepts only `PASS` with no transformation and a result quantity exactly
  equal to submitted fixture quantity; it never silently resizes.
- `FOK`: pre-scan eligible unconsumed depth; insufficient depth produces zero
  fills and exact expiry. Partial FOK is impossible and must fail closed.
- `IOC`: fill zero or more eligible levels immediately, then expire the exact
  remainder. Full fill is `FILLED`.
- `GTC`: fill the immediately marketable portion; rest the remainder as
  `NEW`/`PARTIALLY_FILLED` until explicit trade evidence or cancel effect.
- `MARKET`: consume only explicit eligible depth; insufficient depth may
  partially fill then expire. No infinite or synthetic liquidity.
- Passive fill requires explicit trade-through evidence plus an injected
  queue-ahead estimate. Quote removal alone is not a fill. Trades consume
  queue-ahead before the twin order; queue-cancel benefit stays unavailable
  unless separately calibrated.
- Preserve `ACKNOWLEDGED`, `NEW`, partial fill, terminal and reconciled/local
  observation axes separately. ACK is not fill.

### Status, filters, rate and latency

- Only `TRADING` admits a new synthetic placement. `CANCEL_ONLY` admits only a
  cancel fixture; halt/break/end-of-day/unknown blocks new placement.
- Filter, status, reference or book changes before venue arrival affect the
  result. Missing/expired/unsupported evidence is named and fail-closed.
- Reuse injected L03 rate-budget capacities and observation laws. Exhausted
  capacity blocks the synthetic arrival; 429 observes injected cooldown;
  418 opens manual circuit posture. No second global limiter.
- Latency profiles use bounded injected integer milliseconds for
  dispatch-to-venue, venue-to-HTTP and venue-to-private delivery. At identical
  times use explicit source sequence; absent contradictory ordering is
  `AMBIGUOUS`, not optimistic.
- Private-before-HTTP and HTTP-before-private delivery order must converge.
  Timeout/5xx/disconnect remains locally `UNKNOWN` and cannot trigger a second
  placement.

### Cancel, crash and recovery simulation

- Cancel effect occurs at venue arrival, not request time. Fill-before-cancel
  may win; cancel-before-trade prevents later fill. Dropped cancel response is
  `UNKNOWN` and requires injected reconciliation.
- L07 may model a replacement as a distinct explicit fixture but must not
  claim or simulate the currently contract-gapped cancel-replace API as an
  accepted L02/L06 operation.
- Inject crash before venue arrival, after arrival/before ACK, after partial
  fill, during cancel and after terminal-before-local-observation.
- Crash drops configured local deliveries/commands without erasing an
  already-reached venue effect. Restart remains `RECOVERY_REQUIRED` until an
  injected owner-reconciliation observation is supplied.
- Restore must not duplicate depth consumption, fills, client IDs or event
  delivery.

### Fees, costs and calibration seam

- Accept exact versioned maker/taker fee evidence. Each fill retains quantity,
  price, quote notional, liquidity role, fee amount and fee asset.
- If fee asset is not quote and no exact conversion evidence exists,
  quote-equivalent fee is `null`, never zero.
- Separate spread/depth impact, latency movement, fee and adverse-selection
  observations. Funding/borrow/recovery remain explicit injected external
  owner inputs; L07 does not invent them.
- Emit a versioned calibration comparison from predicted versus injected
  recorded outcome revisions. L07 may validate lineage and compute exact
  residuals; it may not train, fit, store, promote or automatically change a
  model.
- Missing, stale, incompatible or insufficient calibration evidence produces
  `UNAVAILABLE`/`null`; never fabricate probability or confidence.

## Deterministic tests

- Determinism, permutation stability, deep freeze, content/hash sensitivity,
  snapshot/restore and exact resource boundaries.
- Hostile proxy/accessor/inheritance/symbol/sparse/unknown/secret-shaped data
  rejection without invoking attacker-controlled code.
- Buy/sell multi-level depth, exact VWAP and property proof that total fills
  never exceed order quantity or consumed explicit depth.
- FOK sufficient/insufficient atomicity; IOC zero/partial/full expiry; GTC
  partial/rest/cancel; MARKET insufficient-depth partial expiry.
- Passive book removal produces no fill; explicit trade evidence consumes
  queue ahead before the twin order.
- ACK/private permutations, duplicate idempotency and same-ID conflict.
- Timeout/5xx/disconnect local `UNKNOWN` despite possible oracle fill; no
  resubmission.
- Status matrix including `CANCEL_ONLY`; filter/status/book changes during
  latency; no silent transformation.
- L03 rate exhaustion, 429/418, cooldown and clock-regression boundaries.
- Fill/cancel races and dropped cancel response.
- Crash/restart at every durable phase with no duplicate consumption/fill and
  an explicit reconciliation barrier.
- Exact maker/taker fee, unknown conversion `null`, and separated cost axes.
- Calibration revision mismatch, stale/insufficient evidence and missing-value
  behavior.
- Static/import purity: no network, fs, env, clock, timer, randomness,
  service/client, signer, Ledger, Foundation execution or authority-bearing
  output.

## Acceptance

- Same seed, model revision and event set produce identical canonical state,
  scenario and outcome hashes.
- Conservative mode never overstates fills or reuses displayed liquidity.
- Observable and venue-oracle truth remain separate; ambiguity survives until
  exact injected owner reconciliation.
- TIF, latency, cancel, crash, rate and calibration fixtures are bounded,
  explicit and network-free.
- Every artifact is synthetic-only, non-sendable and non-authoritative.
- Focused, accumulated, frozen protocol, standard and full gates pass.
- Adversarial corrections, implementation commit, immutable receipt and state
  transition are complete before L08 begins.
