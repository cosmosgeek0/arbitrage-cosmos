- Preserve the legacy `PREFLIGHT_EVIDENCE` event and
  `evaluateExchangeTwinInstrumentPreflight` behavior. An old Spot LIMIT event
  must still fail closed through the accepted L04 one-argument preflight.
- Add one closed, explicit `SPOT_ORDER_TIF_PREFLIGHT_EVIDENCE` event. It may
  carry only the fixture identity, current L04 registry snapshot, complete
  sizing request and the expected content-addressed L08.1 capability revision.
  It must never accept a caller-supplied result or capability.
- The event is valid only for an exact Spot `LIMIT` fixture with `GTC`, `IOC`
  or `FOK`. Spot `MARKET` remains on the legacy L04 path; USD-M remains on its
  accepted path.
- Rerun `bindSpotOrderTifCapability` and
  `validateSpotOrderTifCapability` at ingestion, venue arrival and checkpoint
  replay. Bind the request's injected evaluation time exactly to the twin
  venue time.
- Retain a discriminated preflight-history union without changing the legacy
  entry shape, top-level state shape, order fixture schema or accepted fill
  mechanics.
- A pass requires exact expected/current capability revision, product,
  instrument, source, registry and snapshot revisions, symbol/assets/side,
  order type/TIF, price, requested/result quantity, notional arithmetic,
  exclusive validity and non-send authority.
- Require requested quantity, filtered result quantity and twin fixture
  quantity to be identical. L08.2 does not silently reinterpret an L04 sizing
  transformation.
- Preserve the existing venue failure projection: any missing, rejected,
  stale, mismatched or tampered capability remains `PREFLIGHT_NOT_EXACT` with

## Current official-source check

Official Binance sources rechecked on `2026-07-13`:

- `https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-spot-trading/api/rest-api/trade`
  lists Spot `LIMIT` with mandatory TIF, quantity and price, and TIF values
  `GTC`, `IOC`, `FOK`.
- `https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/products/spot/enums` defines GTC as
  resting until canceled, IOC as immediate partial-fill then expiry, and FOK
  as full immediate fill or expiry.
- `https://developers.binance.com/en/docs/catalog/core-trading-spot-trading/api/rest-api/general`
  keeps public exchange information and symbol order types separate from the
  documentary TIF contract.

These current semantics corroborate the accepted L08.1 pins; they do not
widen or repin that owner contract.
