# L08 — production order-policy engine

Status: `COMPLETE`

## Purpose

Build a pure, deterministic policy engine that chooses execution mechanics for
an already-authorized, versioned intent. It compares passive/aggressive,
`GTC`/`IOC`/`FOK`, bounded price, escalation, cancel/replace, multi-leg
sequence, maximum naked duration and recovery-cost alternatives without
creating an order, bypassing Capital/Risk, or calling an exchange.

Acceptance requires one explicit versioned non-sendable plan or one named
rejection. The policy may select among mechanics inside existing authorization;
it may never widen quantity, price, notional, loss, duration, capital or risk
limits.

## Authority and integration boundary

- The policy consumes a closed already-authorized intent and exact Capital/
  Risk constraint evidence. It cannot create, approve, reserve, renew, revoke
  or increase an intent, mandate, plan, ticket, position or liability.
- Existing Basis/Volume engines own strategy; Capital owns allocation and
  reservations; Foundation owns risk, execution and reconciliation; L03 owns
  request/rate ambiguity; L04 owns static filters/sizing; L06 owns mutation
  identity; L07 owns synthetic exchange behavior. L08 compares mechanics only.
- Every output is deeply frozen, bounded and carries
  `policyAuthority=ADVISORY_EXECUTION_MECHANICS_ONLY`,
  `simulationAuthority=SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE`,
  `sendEligible=false`, `actionAuthority=NONE_RECORD_ONLY` and
  `externalActionAuthorization=NONE`.
- A plan is documentary input for later director-owned lifecycle composition.
  It is not an order request, send permit, execution ticket, retry instruction,
  canonical risk verdict or cockpit truth.

## Required implementation

### Closed versioned policy input

- Define one exact schema for an authorized-intent projection with intent,
  authorization, mandate, Capital/Risk decision, product/account realm,
  instrument, book/source cut, current order/position/liability, cost/model,
  recovery-budget and expiry revisions.
- Require exact injected evaluation time. Every temporal dependency has an
  explicit observation and exclusive validity bound; stale or future-skewed
  evidence invalidates policy before candidate comparison.
- Preserve exact decimal strings and closed units. No binary floating-point,
  ambient clock, timers, filesystem, environment, sockets, network or
  randomness in policy code.
- Reject unknown fields, aliases, cycles, proxies, accessors, symbols, sparse
  arrays, unsafe text/numbers, secret-shaped data and resource overflow without
  invoking caller code.

### Candidate mechanics

- Compare only currently documented and L04-proved order/TIF subsets. A
  candidate explicitly states product, side, order type, TIF, limit/market
  posture, requested quantity, filtered quantity, price bound and expiry.
- Passive and aggressive candidates remain distinct. Do not encode a fixed
  “maker is cheaper” rule: every candidate must carry exact current fee,
  opportunity-decay, adverse-selection, latency and recovery evidence.
- `IOC`, `FOK` and `GTC` semantics must remain product/filter compatible.
  Unsupported maker-only, price-match, STP or cancel-replace behavior is
  rejected rather than inferred from another product or route.
- Escalation and cancel/replace steps are explicit new planned actions with
  separate identities, deadlines and failure transitions. A timeout,
  disconnect or 5xx never authorizes a blind retry or duplicate placement.
- Price bounds are side-correct and cannot exceed the authorized slippage/
  notional/risk envelope. Quantity after filters cannot exceed requested or
  authorized quantity and cannot silently increase to meet a minimum.

### Distribution comparison and objective

- Accept a bounded exact discrete distribution for each candidate/action:
  probability mass in integer PPM summing exactly to `1_000_000`, fill by
  deadline, filled quantity, fee/rebate, adverse selection, opportunity decay,
  cancel/race cost, unhedged exposure duration, recovery cost and loss.
- Distributions are injected versioned evidence, never fabricated by L08.
  Missing, stale, incompatible, uncalibrated or sample-insufficient evidence
  produces a named rejection or unavailable candidate.
- Compute exact expected filled quantity, expected cost/loss and tail loss/
  CVaR at an explicit closed confidence level. Preserve numerator/denominator
  evidence or fixed exact scales; never convert to binary floating point.
- Select the best conservative authorized objective only after hard price,
  quantity, notional, exposure, duration, loss, recovery-budget, Capital and
  Risk limits pass. Deterministic tie-breaks are explicit and order-independent.
- If uncertainty, tail loss or recovery cost breaches the mandate, return a
  named rejection instead of forcing an order.

### Multi-leg sequence and naked-duration law

- Model ordered and concurrent plans for an already-authorized pair of legs.
  Preserve exact leg identities, products, account realms, side and reduce-only
  posture; do not create a new Basis/Volume coordinator.
- A sequential second-leg quantity depends on actual first-leg fill evidence,
  never requested quantity or ACK. A policy plan may state that dependency but
  cannot observe or execute it.
- Bound maximum one-leg/naked quantity, notional and duration. Candidate
  distributions that exceed any bound are rejected even when their expected
  value is favorable.
- Concurrent plans must name partial-fill imbalance and per-leg ambiguity
  transitions. Ordered plans must name first-leg reject/partial/timeout and the
  exact stop/recovery posture for the other leg.
- Recovery paths are risk-reducing documentary alternatives constrained by
  the injected recovery cost/loss budget. Kill/revoke posture blocks new
  exposure but cannot be rewritten by the policy into authority.

### Output plan and rejection

- The accepted plan contains an exact version/schema, plan ID/revision,
  source intent/authorization/mandate/Capital/Risk/evidence revisions,
  ordered or concurrent leg plan, candidate metrics, selected objective,
  order/TIF/price/quantity terms, timeout/escalation/cancel plan, per-leg
  failure transitions, recovery path, expiry and content hashes.
- Plan and candidate identity are exact content commitments. Equivalent input
  and candidate sets produce byte-identical output regardless of insertion
  order; same-ID content conflicts fail closed.
- Rejections use one closed reason vocabulary and retain no order-like action
  authority. Unknown values remain `null`/`UNKNOWN`/`UNAVAILABLE`, never zero,
  success, readiness or probability.
- Checkpoint/replay is not a new L08 store. If retained comparison evidence is
  needed for deterministic tests, it remains a bounded immutable value only.

## Deterministic tests

- Same input/candidate set and every insertion permutation produce one exact
  plan/rejection identity; seed is absent because policy selection is not
  randomized.
- Exact decimal and PPM arithmetic, mass sum, expected quantity/cost/loss and
  CVaR boundaries including exact confidence-tail cuts.
- Passive wins only with better conservative distribution; aggressive wins
  when wait/decay/adverse/recovery tails dominate; no maker-cost shortcut.
- `GTC`/`IOC`/`FOK`, side-correct price bound and filtered-quantity boundaries;
  unsupported product/TIF/cancel-replace combinations fail closed.
- Stale book/filter/cost/model/authorization and exact expiry equality block.
- Capital/Risk/mandate bounds cannot be increased, omitted, relabeled or
  overridden by favorable expected value.
- Ordered/concurrent two-leg candidates, first-leg partial/reject/timeout,
  second-leg actual-fill dependency, naked quantity/duration and recovery-cost
  comparisons.
- Timeout/5xx/disconnect/cancel-replace ambiguity never creates a retry or
  duplicate action identity.
- Tail-loss/CVaR and recovery-budget exact pass/equality/fail boundaries.
- Missing fee/fill/latency/adverse/decay/recovery values stay unavailable and
  never become zero or confidence.
- Hostile graph, secret-shaped input, duplicate/conflicting identity, bounds,
  deep-freeze, import-purity and no-action-authority proofs.
- Direct static proof that L08 has no exchange client, signer, private read,
  credential, transport, timer, persistence, Foundation/Capital mutation,
  canonical ticket/coordinator or money path.

## Acceptance

- Capital/Risk/mandate constraints cannot be widened or overridden.
