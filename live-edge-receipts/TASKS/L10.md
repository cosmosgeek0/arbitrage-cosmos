## Purpose

Build one deterministic, network-free reverse-Basis liability overlay that binds
the existing Basis/Capital/Risk/L08 identities, consumes accepted L07 order
states as synthetic mechanics evidence, and preserves a distinct injected
Margin identity for borrow, sale, cover, liability and repay truth.

## Required lifecycle

1. fail closed unless borrow quote, inventory, rate/timing, account realm,
   collateral posture and owner gates are current and complete;
2. confirm a realm-bound borrow transaction before any sale can count;
3. persist principal and accrued interest as explicit liability truth;
4. sell no more than confirmed principal through the same injected Margin realm;
5. hedge actual accepted L07 Margin-sale mechanics fill quantity in USD-M;
6. supervise exact principal, interest, collateral, order and hedge drift truth;
7. cover the actual Margin short, close the actual Futures long and repay;
8. require fresh exact-zero liability plus exact reconciliation before `CLOSED`
   or `RECOVERED`;
9. retain `RECOVERY_REQUIRED` or `MANUAL_INTERVENTION` while any debt, open
   order, ambiguous mutation, unmatched fill or exposure remains.
10. retain the initial liability, exact pre-repay liability basis, each
    order's first/last event-envelope time, each recovery-evidence occurrence
    time and full preflight owner evidence in checkpoint state;
11. freeze one exposure-bound recovery evidence batch before any recovery
    order and require both risk-reducing recovery legs to consume that batch.

## Hard boundary

- No Margin, Spot, USD-M, signed, private, test-order or network call.
- No signer, credential, environment, socket, timer, filesystem or wall clock.
- No transfer, universal transfer, internal transfer, withdrawal, leverage or
  Margin-mode mutation path.
- L07 `SPOT` is `SPOT_MARKET_MECHANICS_ONLY`, never a Margin action identity.
- All outputs remain `SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE`, `NONE_RECORD_ONLY`, non-sendable and
  externally unauthorized.

## Focused acceptance

- stale/missing/insufficient borrow blocks;
- borrow confirmed then Margin sale rejected preserves liability and requires
  recovery;
- partial sale hedges only actual fill;
- accrued interest changes total liability exactly;
- partial cover, repay rejection/ambiguity and residual dust cannot close;
- restart with liability preserves the same deterministic state/revision;
- injected external manual repayment can close only after exact reconciliation;
- any open order, L07 ambiguity/recovery, ambiguous mutation or unmatched fill
  prevents `CLOSED`/`RECOVERED`;
- no forbidden imports or external-effect primitives exist in the L10 source;
- rehashed state cannot widen quantities beyond confirmed principal, substitute
  zero/regressing order liability, or hide gate/collateral expiry after borrow;
- focused, accumulated liveEdge, protocol, `npm test` and `npm run test:all`
  gates report real exit status.
