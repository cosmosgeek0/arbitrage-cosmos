## Deliverable

and emits a bounded, immutable L12 snapshot for later System Integration
composition. It must not become another Capital, Risk, execution,
reconciliation, recovery, persistence or supervisor owner.

The adapter must:

- preserve one exact subject across proposal, mandate, reservation, approval,
  risk, execution, exposure and reconciliation evidence;
- bind reservation, position, capacity, outstanding, execution,
  reconciliation and recovery revisions into one canonical truth scope;
- require a current Foundation Risk verdict immediately before the exact
  mutation attempt represented by injected evidence;
- validate the §19 halt matrix: increases stay blocked while observation,
  reconciliation, cancel, reduce, close, hedge, unwind, repay, recovery and
  ledger reads remain available to their owners;
- copy the complete §20 exposure vector only from confirmed-fill and
  reconciled Capital truth, never requested quantities or caller prices;
- retain funding, borrow cost, fees, realized/unrealized PnL and capacity as
  opaque Capital-owned values; unknown remains `null`;
- preserve explicit orphans, outstanding mutations, manual activity, residual
  exposure and residual liability;
- start cold reconstruction `HALTED` or `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`, enforce the seven
  ordered prerequisites, and advance no further than owner review;
- accept a closed structural evidence envelope for all 20 recovery scenarios,
  require stable mutation identities under replay, behavior-test every valid
  durable L10 crash cut, exercise the canonical Foundation coordinator across
  the exact 15-plan-state and 10-leg-state lifecycle unions, and reject a
  terminal state that hides debt, exposure or outstanding work;
- remain synthetic, non-sendable, non-authorizing and unwired.
