## Required command contract

One closed discriminated set covers:

- component desired state, pause and resume intake;
- bounded configuration update;
- approval, arm, disarm, re-arm and revocation;
- halt and reconciliation;
- cancel, close and reduce;
- execution of a precomputed recovery plan and liability repayment;
- safe component restart; and
- awareness-only recovery acknowledgement.

Every action must have an exact bounded schema. Generic payload bags and
client-supplied actor identity are forbidden. Types absent at accepted L06 are
versioned opaque `WAITING_L08` references and cannot be dispatched.

## Command law

- Resolve authenticated operator identity through an injected server port.
- Generate, digest and authenticate the preview server-side.
- Bind command ID, action, actor, config/control versions, risk evidence and
  expiry to canonical bytes.
- Reserve the optimistic global control revision through an exact injected
  persistence acknowledgement before owner publication.
- Record closed intent, applied, awareness and rejection event shapes through
  the injected System Integration Ledger port; no direct Ledger edit.
- Make exact completed and terminal-rejected replays idempotent; conflicting
  command intent fails closed.
- Treat any post-intent uncertain owner/persistence/deadline result as pending
  `COMMAND_OUTCOME_UNCERTAIN`, never a false terminal rejection or success.
- Re-read injected exchange truth and canonical Foundation Risk for
  `CANCEL`/`CLOSE` and other risk-bounded recovery actions. Reject any projected
  increase in the predeclared exact-decimal measure.
- `ACKNOWLEDGE_RECOVERY` records awareness only and always reports
  `recoveryStateChanged=false`.
- All event/receipt shapes retain `externalActionAuthorization=NONE`.

## Launch §7 controls amendments

- No command may promote the fixed tiny-live capital envelope or bypass

- No deployment-region claim or latency benchmark is created.
- Regime posture remains advisory through existing owners; no regime
  supervisor is added.
- Runtime recording health remains independent from money-critical control,
  reconciliation and recovery truth.
