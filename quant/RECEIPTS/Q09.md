## Delivered boundary

Q09 adds the §13.4/§14.5(baseline)/§14.7 change, regime, anomaly and analog machinery
as a pure deterministic library (`quant/src/quant_edge/regime/**`) and no operational
producer, market feature family, trained model, calibration or authority:

- quality-gated observation contracts: stale input freezes sequential state, every
  gap-class quality resets it visibly, missing never becomes zero and warmup carries
  no alarm authority;
- two-sided CUSUM with explicit slack/threshold, fixed or warmup-estimated reference,
  and a restart-after-alarm law under which a persistent level shift raises one alarm,
  not an alarm every bar;
- two-sided Page-Hinkley against the exact integer running mean quantized half-even
  per step (the quantization law is the statistic's definition, so replay is
  drift-free by construction);
- offline PELT over a declared quantized SSE objective (one half-even rounding per
  segment-cost evaluation, then exact integer dynamic programming with a documented
  pruning slack of 2 restoring the pruning theorem under quantization); property
  campaigns prove exact parity with brute-force optimal partitioning;
- bounded-run-length BOCPD (Adams-MacKay) over a robust median/MAD alphabet: exact
  Dirichlet(1)-categorical predictive quantized half-even, fixed-point run-length
  posterior under a largest-remainder normalization law, cap-bin mass folding, a
  cumulative short-run-mass alarm statistic and an explicit post-reset alarm warmup;
  measured against an exact-Fraction reference of the same model, the posterior
  deviates by at most 1 ulp of 10^-12 across a planted regime shift;
- a soft-regime forward filter behind the stable `RegimeModel` protocol: exact
  fixed-point transition/emission rows, strictly positive emissions, and a
  floor-mixture law that keeps every state probability at or above the declared floor
  (the §14.6 unseen-regime overconfidence guard, enforced by construction); hard
  labels are exposed only alongside the exact soft distribution;
- the robust MAD/quantile anomaly baseline with explicit `UNCALIBRATED_SCORE`
  semantics, unbounded-surprise truth for zero-MAD deviations, persistence runs, and
  a worst-of disorder/OOD fusion (`NORMAL`/`WATCH`/`DISORDERED`/`OOD_SUSPECT`/
  `UNKNOWN`) with sorted reason codes that yields `UNKNOWN` on ineligible input;
- an earlier-only left Matrix Profile: candidates must end at or before the query
  start minus the embargo, ranking is exact integer correlation-order comparison
  (signed rho-squared cross-multiplication; no floats, no square roots), exact ties
  keep the earliest window, similarity is half-even-quantized signed rho-squared, and
  every ordered window pair lands in exactly one measured exclusion bucket
  (`FUTURE`/`OVERLAP`/`SAME_EVENT`/`DEGENERATE`) — a conservation law proved by
  property tests;
- earlier-only top-`k` analog retrieval with symbol/regime cohort filters, the
  cutoff/embargo leakage boundary, overlap/same-event/shape/degenerate exclusion
  counters, deterministic ties (earlier end time, then record identity), outcome
  distributions over the retrieved cohort (never cherry-picked examples) and honest
  `NO_ANALOGS`/`NO_QUERY_SIGNAL` degradation;
- a typed measurement harness: detection reports with exact false-alarm rates over
  in-control bars, per-change delays, missed/redundant partitions and fail-closed
  budget verdicts; segmentation boundary-error reports; anomaly hit/false

## Adversarial review and corrections

1. CUSUM with a warmup-estimated reference re-alarmed on every bar after a persistent
   level shift (the frozen reference never adapted); corrected to a restart-after-alarm
   law that re-estimates a warmup-based reference from post-alarm data, with golden and
   unit regressions (seeded false alarms fell from 2,047 to 0).
2. BOCPD never alarmed on `posterior[0]` alone at realistic hazards because change
   evidence accumulates across several short run lengths; corrected to a cumulative
   short-run-mass statistic plus an explicit post-reset alarm warmup that silences the
   initial delta-at-zero prior artifact (seeded detections rose from 0/33 to 33/33
   with zero false alarms).
3. The Matrix Profile visited diagonals in ascending lag order, so exact correlation
   ties kept the latest window while the documentation and the analog module promise
   the earliest; corrected to descending-lag traversal with an exact-tie regression
   test; committed goldens regenerated.
4. A CUSUM both-sides-alarm comparison branch was proved unreachable (pre-step
   statistics sum below twice the threshold and one side always shrinks) and removed
   with the proof documented; the Page-Hinkley equivalent is reachable through warmup
   accumulation and is covered by direction tests in both orders.
5. PELT segment-cost and objective contracts silently capped values at int64 while
   lawful quantized costs exceed it; explicit `MAX_COST`/`MAX_PENALTY` bounds added
   with a huge-penalty regression test.
6. Matrix Profile pair accounting dropped pairs whose query window was degenerate;
   corrected to a fixed exclusion precedence in which every ordered pair lands in
   exactly one measured bucket, with the conservation law asserted by unit and
   property tests.
7. The anomaly step contract wrongly forbade a suppressed step from echoing frozen
   persistence; corrected so only OK steps must agree exactly.

Final review outcome: no remaining P0-P3 findings known to the author; independent
exact-commit verification required before acceptance.

## Limitations stated honestly

- Every Q09 proof is `SYNTHETIC`; no detector, regime, anomaly score or analogue has
  been computed on real market data and none is claimed predictive, calibrated or
  economically valuable. Measured false-alarm/delay numbers characterize the fixtures,
  not markets.
- ruptures/STUMPY/River/PyOD/FAISS/hmmlearn remain deferred exactly as the Q01
  framework cards record; Q09's exact reference implementations are the parity
  baselines those cards demand. ADWIN/KSWIN remain deferred with River.
- The BOCPD cap bin sees at most `run_cap` history symbols, a documented
  bounded-run-length approximation; hazard is a fixed constant, not learned.
- The v1 regime specification's transition/emission tables are machinery constants;
  learned parameters, HSMM durations and per-regime calibration belong to Q10+/Q12.
- PELT optimizes its declared quantized objective exactly; the quantization is a
  documented engineering bound (half a cost unit per segment evaluation), not a
  statistical claim.
- Matrix Profile and analog retrieval are exact and bounded (2,048 points / 256-wide
  windows / 10,000 records); FAISS-scale approximate retrieval is a later challenger.
- Volatility-triggered bars remain deferred: this layer provides the change-point
  trigger seam, but bar construction lives in the Q06-frozen feature module, so the
  wiring is a later integration change, not a shard write.
