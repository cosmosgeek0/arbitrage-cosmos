## Delivered boundary

Q11 adds the §14.4 survival and competing-risk layer as a pure deterministic library
(`quant/src/quant_edge/survival/**`) and no operational producer, market feature
family, trained market model, live calibration claim or authority:

- censored-subject contracts: `EVENT`-with-cause or `RIGHT_CENSORED` with the Q05
  shape law (a cause exists if and only if the status is `EVENT`); a quality-gated
  cohort builder that excludes gap-class subjects into measured per-quality counts
  (§11.7: gapped evidence is never a normal negative; censored cases remain censored
  structurally — no path imputes an event or drops censoring silently);
- exact Kaplan-Meier and Nelson-Aalen over integer risk-set/event counts, computed in
  unbounded exact rational arithmetic and quantized half-even to fixed point exactly
  once at the contract boundary; events precede censorings at tied times; step
  evaluation beyond observed follow-up returns `None` (unknowns stay `UNKNOWN`);
- the reverse-KM censoring-survival estimator through the same machinery, with the
  tied-time risk set excluding that time's true events;
- Aalen-Johansen cause-specific cumulative incidence with the exact conservation law
  `S(t) + sum_k CIF_k(t) == 1` proved by property tests over an independent exact
  reference (never assumed); single-cause incidence equals `1 - KM` exactly;
- a stratified discrete-time cause-specific hazard model (§14.1 transparent baseline)
  whose complete fitted state is integer sufficient statistics; documented Laplace law
  `h_k = (d_k + a)/(n + (K+1)a)` over the bin outcome simplex (sums to one exactly);
  right-closed binning with in-bin censoring treated as censoring at bin end;
  beyond-grid durations administratively censored at grid end and counted; unseen
  strata and bins below the declared minimum risk set yield explicit `UNKNOWN`
  predictions (KNOWN bins form a proven prefix); with an event-aligned grid and no
  smoothing the model's survival equals KM exactly (life-table equivalence,
  property-tested);
- time-dependent metrics in exact arithmetic over quantized predictions: IPCW Brier
  `BS(t)` (Graf weights: events `1/G(T-)`, survivors `1/G(t)`, censored-by-`t` zero
  and counted) with the documented step-integration law for IBS; same-cohort IPCW
  weights are structurally positive (a subject needing a weight at `t` sat in every
  earlier censoring risk set without being censored), and the report contract keeps
  an explicit `UNAVAILABLE` shape for future externally estimated censoring curves;
  Harrell concordance with half-credit score ties, optional horizon truncation,
  cause scoping and the §11.7 same-group pair exclusion law (excluded and counted);
  censoring-aware calibration reports whose per-bin observed frequencies are KM/AJ
  estimates over the bin cohort, with `EMPTY`/`UNAVAILABLE` bins explicit and the
  expected calibration error over evaluable members only; a declared
  `SurvivalBudgetV1` turning measurements into fail-closed `PASS`/`FAIL` verdicts
  where unavailability fails;
- the §14.4 v1 target vocabulary (time to ignition/continuation/fade/convergence/
  invalidation and the actionability window) binding each target to its primary and
  competing causes as machinery constants, not market truth;
- a deterministic campaign (`python -m quant_edge.survival.campaign`) writing the
  committed golden fixture and seeded measurement evidence under
  `quant/generated/q11/` byte-identically across processes.

No module opens sockets, polls REST, reads env/secrets/ambient clocks, writes
operational storage, imports production clients or conveys any authority; floats are
excluded from all public numeric contracts (internal exactness is `fractions.Fraction`,
quantized once at the boundary per the documented law).

## Adversarial review and corrections

1. The seeded campaign model with Laplace `alpha = 1` was measurably miscalibrated
   (held-out any-cause ECE 0.222268 at grid end, seeded budget verdict `FAIL`):
   with ~40-60 at risk per bin, adding one pseudo-event per cause per bin inflates
   low-hazard strata by ~2% per bin per cause over 12 bins. Corrected to
   `alpha = 1/100` with the rationale documented in the campaign source (ECE fell
   to 0.028710 and the verdict is honestly `PASS`); the golden model keeps
   `alpha = 1` so the smoothing law itself stays exercised and hand-verified.
2. The planned zero-censoring-survival fail-closed branch inside `evaluate_brier`
   was proved structurally unreachable for same-cohort reverse-KM weights (any
   subject needing a weight at `t` was in every earlier censoring risk set without
   being censored, so every factor of `G` it depends on is positive); the dead
   branch was removed, the positivity argument documented in source, and the
   `UNAVAILABLE` report shape retained and contract-tested for future externally
   estimated censoring curves.
3. `v1_target_spec` originally ended in an unreachable no-spec `raise` after a
   linear scan; replaced with a total dict lookup built from the spec table so no
   dead error path ships.
4. Early step-lookup helpers exited loops with `break`, leaving provably one-sided
   branch arcs; rewritten as total scans so 100% branch coverage is real, not
   excluded.
5. Campaign-internal fail-closed guards (`_require_covered`, model prediction rows
   over UNKNOWN strata, marginal predictions beyond follow-up) were extracted to
   module level and unit-tested in both directions instead of living as untestable
   inline raises at the fixed seed.
6. An initial metrics test fixture wrongly assumed reverse-KM `G` could reach zero
   while a later survivor still existed; the fixture (not the library) was wrong —
   replaced with the structural-positivity regression test plus hand-built
   `UNAVAILABLE` report shapes for the budget's unavailability reasons.

Final review outcome: no remaining P0-P3 findings known to the author; independent
exact-commit verification required before acceptance.

## Limitations stated honestly

- Every Q11 proof is `SYNTHETIC`; no estimator or model has been fitted to real
  market data and none is claimed predictive, calibrated on markets or economically
  valuable. The measured recovery gaps, Brier scores, concordances and calibration
  errors characterize the fixtures and the known synthetic generating law, not
  markets.
- Cox partial likelihood, parametric AFT and gradient-boosted survival (XGBoost
  AFT) remain deferred challengers exactly as the Q01 framework cards record
  (scikit-survival is `REJECT` on license/compiled-surface grounds); Q11's exact
  estimators and the discrete-time hazard model are the transparent parity
  baselines those challengers must beat on identical cohorts at Q10+/Q12.
- The discrete-time model conditions on a single bounded stratum id; continuous
  covariates, interactions and learned stratifications belong to the later model
  battery, not this layer.
- The binning law (in-bin censoring at bin end) is a documented discrete-time
  convention: it makes life-table and KM equal on aligned grids but is a coarse
  approximation for grids much wider than the underlying event resolution.
- Left truncation and time-varying covariates are out of scope for v1; subjects
  enter at their own time origin only.
- IPCW structural positivity holds for same-cohort reverse-KM weights only; an
  externally estimated censoring curve must use the report contract's
  `UNAVAILABLE` shape and fail closed.
- The six v1 target specs are a versioned vocabulary proving the layer end-to-end;
  real cohorts arrive from the Q05 label factory at integration time, and no
  target spec asserts market frequencies or hazard shapes.
