## Delivered design

Q12 adds one bounded, deterministic, advisory-only combination layer:

1. A complete Q14-bound OOF matrix and exact convex grid stack. The matrix retains and
   revalidates its samples, plan, fold membership, specialist semantics, target,
   horizon and feature times. The fitted model identity binds the complete fitted stack.
2. Chronological Platt and exact-isotonic calibration. Fit, evaluation and application
   are derived from explicit ordered folds. Public consumers canonically refit the
   calibrator, so altered coefficients, knots, support or source evidence fail closed.
3. An empirical fixed-width canonical-stack split-conformal baseline. This is not
   learned quantile regression or a CQR claim. Every base interval is the canonical
   stack score plus/minus exact `1/5`; model, coverage, assessment and adaptive state
   bind the same matrix, fitted stack and width. Public apply requires recomputable
   model evidence.
4. A bounded adaptive prequential challenger that predicts before outcome visibility,
   accepts only identity-bound mature updates, and preserves unknown outcomes without
   fabricating state.
5. Named uncertainty proxies and a final bound advisory decision. Source quality,
   canonical Q09 disorder, specialist disagreement, empirical outcome dispersion,
   interval width, and Q14 cost/timing stress remain separate. Nested bound evidence
   and the complete context are recomputed at public decision boundaries.
6. Additive closure of all five Q10 `WAITING_Q14` records through Q14 public owners.
   Holdout/quarantine rows remain excluded from research fitting, leakage and stress;
   accepted Q10 evidence is not rewritten.

The only outputs are `WATCH` and `ABSTAIN_UNKNOWN`. `WATCH` is advisory evidence and
cannot authorize an order. Stale, OOD, specialist conflict, missing cost, known
cost-negative, missing calibration, unavailable interval, or unknown/collapsed
coverage independently prevents `WATCH`.
