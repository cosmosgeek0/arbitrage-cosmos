## Adversarial review and corrections

1. **Holdout contamination of the leakage verdict** — `compare_models` originally
   assessed leakage over the full sample set, letting holdout outcomes influence a
   pre-holdout decision; fixed by restricting assessment (and fold metrics) to a
   research-only subset with new `subset_by_ids`/`restrict_predictions` contracts and
   regression tests.
2. Three provably-dead defensive branches (an unreachable probe-value clause in
   `control_probe_ok`, a fold-status re-check made unreachable by the plan-status
   gate in `compare_models`, and order-statistic clamps unreachable under the
   bounded coverage contract) were restructured away rather than papered over.
3. The DSR variance-adjustment gate was made honestly reachable and regression-fixed
   with an exact two-point distribution at the Pearson kurtosis bound
   (4 × 1/10 with 16 × 1/40 → radicand exactly zero), instead of an untestable guard.
4. A hand-computed calibration-slope expectation in a test disagreed with the exact
   implementation; recomputation proved the implementation right (cov 0.2, var 0.41,
   slope 20/41) and the test was corrected — the metric law is exact, the reviewer
   was not.
5. Test-design corrections: an 8-replicate no-skill band was too narrow for the
   comparison fixtures (control-failure rejections of honest models) and the
   implausibility bound had to be direction-appropriate for Brier; both fixed in
   specs, with the conservative rejection posture itself kept and documented.

Known bounded limitation (recorded, not hidden): worst-case PBO compute at the gate
maxima (256 trials × 12,870 splits) is exact but slow; callers own their matrix
sizes. The no-skill envelope is a min/max band and may conservatively reject an
honest model; that reject-when-in-doubt posture is deliberate.

Final review outcome: no remaining P0–P3 findings known to the author; independent
exact-commit verification required before acceptance.
