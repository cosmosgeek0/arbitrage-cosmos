## Adversarial review and corrections

The independent review repeatedly attempted to break acceptance. Every reproduced
path was corrected and retained as a regression test:

1. SIGKILL after begin/before commit restores the exact prior snapshot; SIGKILL
   after commit restores the complete control-run snapshot and retry is idempotent.
2. Caller-forged shadow metrics, scores or outcomes fail because exact fixed-point
   values bind their digests and comparisons reproduce from admitted feedback.
3. Drift cannot self-promote. An abstaining champion rejects promotion, and recovery
   changes generation/transition so pre-drift shadow evidence remains stale.
4. Forged Q14 subject/report binding, evidence-posture upgrades, incomplete gates,
   missing comparison/report/batch/feedback lineage and corrupt artifact/card/model
   rows all fail restart or promotion.
5. Duplicate champion lifecycles, multi-slot occupancy, broken history links,
   forged promotion/demotion/recovery/rollback semantics and backward timestamps
   fail closed.
6. Unrelated artifact hashes cannot retire a model; admitted retirement evidence
   must bind the exact model ID, revision and artifact.
7. Owner annotations cannot cross events or outcomes; hidden tags precede objective
   feedback, known-outcome tags require it, and links follow both timestamps.
8. Producer-neutral compatibility was proven with a generic future envelope on the
   same target/score contract, without importing or naming Q12 implementation code.

Final independent code-review outcome: no remaining implementation blocker
reproduced; only receipt and acceptance procedure remained.
