## Purpose

Build the §12 feature common law: a versioned feature registry whose cards carry every
required field, deterministic sampling-domain/window primitives (wall, event,
trade-count and volume time), robust symbol-relative baselines with explicit context
cohorts and history-sufficiency gates, feature freshness/quality propagation over the
accepted quality vocabulary, and the streaming/batch equivalence harness with declared
integer-ulp tolerances and committed golden vectors. A small set of concrete v1
primitive features proves the machinery end to end. Q06 implements no Q07+
microstructure families, no model, no calibration and no action authority; all
executable proofs are honest `SYNTHETIC` fixtures.

## Code and evidence artifacts

New package `quant/src/quant_edge/features/`:

- **Deterministic fixed-point law.** A single explicit integer fixed-point
  representation with a declared scale and half-even rounding for every multiply/divide,
  so streaming arithmetic is platform-deterministic and bounded; exact rationals remain
  the reference for batch computation.
- **Sampling domains (§12.1).** Deterministic bar construction over evidence series for
  wall/clock time, event time, trade-count time and volume time, each with explicit
  boundary law, empty-bar truth and bounded counts; no one-minute-candle default.
- **Robust baselines (§12.2).** Bounded-window rolling median/MAD, fixed-point EWMA
  mean/variance with an explicit alpha and update law, bounded-window exact quantiles,
  and baseline cohort keys for time-of-day, funding-cycle, regime and liquidity context,
  gated by an explicit listing-age/history-sufficiency contract that yields `UNKNOWN`
  rather than a fabricated baseline.
- **Registry law (§12).** A feature card carrying every required field (name/version,
  economic hypothesis, inputs and exact windows, domain, normalization cohort,
  missing/stale behavior, units and bounds, incremental update rule, batch reference,
  streaming implementation, provenance, computational cost, expected failure modes,
  tests and golden-vector binding) and a registry with sorted unique identities, a
  content digest and a deterministic Markdown projection.
- **Streaming/batch equivalence harness.** For each registered feature: a batch
  reference over the full series and a streaming incremental implementation, compared
  bar-for-bar under the card's declared integer-ulp tolerance; a typed equivalence
  report with max deviation; failure fails the feature, not the tolerance.
- **Freshness and quality propagation.** Feature values carry status
  (`AVAILABLE`/`STALE`/`DATA_INVALID`-mapped/`UNKNOWN` per the accepted vocabulary),
  as-of times and worst-of-inputs quality propagation with explicit freshness bounds;
  missing input never becomes zero.
- **Concrete v1 primitives.** At least four production-shaped primitive features
  (e.g. bar return, rolling activity intensity, robust z-distance against median/MAD,
  EWMA variance) registered with full cards, both implementations and golden vectors.
- **Bounded redundancy diagnostics (§12.3 subset).** Deterministic, model-free
  diagnostics over feature matrices: Pearson and rank correlation, near-constant/
  variance checks, missingness-as-signal counts and greedy correlation clustering.
  Fold-based mutual information, permutation importance, SHAP and ablation are
  explicitly deferred to the model/validation phases and recorded as such.

Evidence artifacts: committed golden fixture(s) under `quant/fixtures/`, hash-bound
generated evidence under `quant/generated/q06/` via a deterministic campaign script,
a decision note `quant/DECISIONS/Q06-001-FEATURE-REGISTRY.md` and a design note
`quant/docs/Q06_FEATURE_REGISTRY_DESIGN.md`.

## Acceptance conditions

- Streaming and batch golden vectors agree within each card's declared tolerance; any
  disagreement marks the feature not production-ready and fails the suite.
- Every registered feature carries the complete §12 card; the registry digest and
  Markdown projection are deterministic and tamper-evident.
- Baselines are context-conditioned and history-gated; insufficient history yields
  `UNKNOWN`, never a fabricated value; missing/stale behavior is explicit per card.
- All sampling domains have explicit boundary laws and empty-bar truth.
- All evidence is honestly `SYNTHETIC`; no real-data feature claim exists; deferred
  §12.3 diagnostics are recorded as deferred, not silently skipped.
- Focused/property/registry/safety/accumulated tests, deterministic replay and
  independent adversarial review pass; immutable `quant/RECEIPTS/Q06.md` exists in
  implementation commit C6; a later independently verified acceptance commit A6
  records C6 and advances state.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- No Q07/Q08 microstructure or derivatives feature families, models, calibration,
  drift, inference or UI.
- No floats in public numeric contracts; no silent rounding; no unbounded windows or
  unbounded registries.
- No missing-as-zero, stale-as-fresh, insufficient-history-as-baseline or
  synthetic-as-market-truth behavior.
- No new external data, sockets, env/secret reads, operational stores or production
  registry mutation; no raw external bytes in Git.
- No claim that a registered feature is predictive, economically valuable or validated;
  Q06 proves machinery only.
