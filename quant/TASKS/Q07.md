## Purpose

Build the §13.1 microstructure/liquidity family library over the evidence contracts the
lane honestly holds: L1 top-of-book evidence with sizes (Binance bookTicker shape),
maker-flagged trades (aggTrades shape) and Q04 book-view ladders. Deliver the §20 Q07

contracts cannot support honestly (e.g. cancellation-versus-execution decomposition
from public diff-depth alone) are `BLOCKED_DATA` with recorded reasons, never faked.

## Code and evidence artifacts

- **Input contracts.** `TopOfBookPointV1` (bid/ask price+size, receive-ordered,
  uncrossed), `TradePointV1` (price, size, `is_buyer_maker`), bounded series containers
  with completeness truth and `SYNTHETIC`/`REPLAY` posture, plus adapters from Q04
  `BookViewV1` ladders for multi-level features.
- **L1 family.** Cont-style L1 order-flow imbalance (documented e_n law over best
  bid/ask price/size transitions), size-weighted microprice and its displacement from
  mid, L1 queue imbalance.
- **Book family.** Band depth totals and cumulative queue imbalance over ladders, book
  slope/convexity per side (exact-rational fits), depth depletion and replenishment
  between consecutive views, a bounded resiliency (recovery-within-k-views) measure,
  and intended-size executable VWAP/spread-at-size by walking the ladder with explicit
  insufficient-depth truth.
- **Flow family.** Maker-flag signed trade imbalance, trade intensity with index of
  dispersion (variance/mean, no square roots), size concentration (HHI), effective
  spread against the prevailing quote, Amihud-style illiquidity per bar and a
  Kyle-style exact-rational OLS slope of mid change on signed flow, plus an absorption
  proxy (aggressive flow with bounded adverse mid response and replenishment evidence).
- **Family dispositions.** A machine-readable §13.1 disposition table with per-bullet
  status and reasons; `possible_spoof_like_pattern` semantics only — no individual
  spoofing claim.
- **Cards, equivalence and benchmarks.** Every implemented feature registers a complete
  Q06 card; streaming/batch equivalence runs under declared integer-ulp tolerances via
  a Q07 item-sequence harness; each streaming implementation reports a deterministic
  per-update operation count, and the campaign script records host wall-time
  measurements as environment-specific evidence (never asserted in tests).

Evidence: committed golden fixture, hash-bound `quant/generated/q07/*` campaign
artifacts, `quant/DECISIONS/Q07-001-MICROSTRUCTURE-LIBRARY.md` and
`quant/docs/Q07_MICROSTRUCTURE_DESIGN.md`.
