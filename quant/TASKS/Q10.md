## Purpose

Build the §14.1/§14.2/§14.3 transparent-and-boosted model battery as a deterministic
advisory research library (`quant_edge.models`): calibrated linear baselines and honest
controls, the Q01-selected boosted challenger family (XGBoost only), and bounded,
fully-logged Optuna studies — all proven on honest `SYNTHETIC` fixtures. Acceptance is
mechanical: every requested trial is logged (including failed ones) and complex
challengers are allowed to lose honestly, with the loss preserved in committed,
hash-bound comparison reports. Nothing here claims a model is predictive, validated or
economically valuable; Q10 proves machinery and measurement only.

## Code and evidence artifacts

New package `quant/src/quant_edge/models/`:

- **Quantization law.** Model outputs are binary64 internally; each public prediction is
  quantized exactly once, half-even, onto the Q06 `FixedPointV1` scale through a single
  documented entry point. No float appears in any public numeric contract or artifact.
  All public metrics are exact rationals of the once-quantized predictions and labels
  (Brier, exact tie-aware AUC, binned ECE, MSE, MAE, pinball, quantile coverage,
  crossing count, precision/recall at `k`, Kendall tau), except log-loss, which is
  explicitly labeled `BINARY64_DERIVED` and quantized once at the metric layer.
- **Synthetic dataset factory.** Deterministic chronological tabular datasets from
  `DeterministicRandom` (seed `20260712` campaigns): declared feature roles (planted
  monotone linear signal, planted nonlinear interaction, pure noise, liquidity/activity
  proxies), integer-valued features/labels, and four targets — nonlinear binary
  transition, noise-dominated linear regression (where the boosted challenger may
  honestly lose), heteroscedastic quantiles, and grouped attention ranking. Posture is
  `SYNTHETIC` only; planted structure is machinery truth, never market truth.
- **Battery-internal chronological split.** One train/validation/test split in row
  order with a declared embargo gap, typed `BATTERY_INTERNAL_NON_AUTHORITATIVE`. It
  exists so studies have an objective and reports have an out-of-train slice; it is not
  purged/embargoed walk-forward, not an untouched holdout, and confers no validation
  verdict.
- **WAITING-Q14 seam.** Purged/embargoed walk-forward, untouched-holdout governance,
  negative-control factory, cost/latency stress and PBO/DSR diagnostics are Q14
  property. No frozen Q14 interface exists at the activation base, so the disposition
  table records those exact pieces `WAITING_Q14`; they are never reimplemented here and
  no Q10 comparison claims validation-grade authority.
- **Transparent baselines and controls (§14.1).** Calibrated logistic regression,
  elastic-net logistic, least-squares and quantile linear regression, liquidity-only and
  activity-only single-feature rankers, deterministic random-rank control and a
  no-trade/prevalence control. Classifier probabilities pass a shared isotonic
  calibration stage fit on the validation slice only; ECE is measured before and after.
- **Boosted challengers (§14.3).** XGBoost binary classifier, squared-error regressor,
  quantile regressor and pairwise ranker behind typed wrappers: `tree_method="hist"`,
  `nthread=1`, explicit seeds, bounded depth/estimators, monotone constraints only on
  features whose planted economic direction makes them defensible by construction (the
  constraint is property-tested). Challengers receive the identical calibration stage.
- **Bounded Optuna studies.** Sequential in-memory studies with seeded TPE over
  finite/discrete parameter grids (exact JSON-safe parameter values), hard trial budget
  per study, objective measured on the validation slice only. Every trial — complete,
  failed or pruned — becomes a typed record; a study whose logged-trial count differs
  from its budget fails closed.
- **Comparison reports.** Per target: every registered baseline, control and challenger
  appears exactly once with its quantized metrics and prediction digest; the winner is
  chosen by the predeclared primary metric; losses (including boosted losses to linear
  baselines) are preserved verbatim. A §14 disposition table records ADOPT/ADAPT/
  REFERENCE_ONLY/REJECT/WAITING_Q14/DATA_INSUFFICIENT status per bullet with reasons.
- **Deterministic campaign.** `python scripts/q10_model_campaign.py OUTPUT_ROOT`
  writes, without network/env/ambient-time access:
  `quant/fixtures/q10_model_golden_v1.json`,
  `quant/generated/q10/model_campaign.v1.json` and `.md`; byte-identical across
  processes. Host wall-time evidence is stdout-only, never in artifacts.

## Acceptance conditions

- Every Optuna study logs exactly its budgeted number of trials, including failed and
  pruned attempts, into typed, hash-bound records; missing-trial states fail closed.
- Transparent baselines exist for classification, regression, quantile and ranking
  targets; classifier probabilities are calibrated (isotonic, validation-slice-only)
  with ECE measured before and after; uncalibrated scores are labeled as such.
- Boosted challengers run under the recorded determinism constraints; monotone
  constraints appear only where the planted direction defends them and are
  property-tested.
- Comparison reports are complete (every registered model exactly once), winners follow
  predeclared primary metrics, and at least one honest boosted loss is preserved in the
  committed fixed-seed artifacts.
- The Q14-owned validation pieces are recorded `WAITING_Q14` and not reimplemented; the
  internal split is typed non-authoritative everywhere it appears.

  property/contract/safety/accumulated tests pass; immutable `quant/RECEIPTS/Q10.md`
