## Purpose

Build the §14.4 survival and competing-risk layer as a deterministic pure library:
censored time-to-event contracts for the six declared targets (time to ignition,
continuation, fade, Basis convergence, invalidation, and the fill/actionability
window), exact Kaplan-Meier / Nelson-Aalen / reverse-KM estimators, the
Aalen-Johansen cause-specific cumulative-incidence estimator with an exact
conservation law, a transparent stratified discrete-time cause-specific hazard model
whose parameters are integer sufficient statistics, and a time-dependent metrics
harness (IPCW Brier score, integrated Brier score, concordance with a same-group
exclusion law, and censoring-aware calibration reports). Survival curves and
time-dependent metrics pass deterministic fixtures; all executable proofs are honest
`SYNTHETIC` fixtures; nothing here claims a model is predictive, validated or
economically valuable.

## Code and evidence artifacts

New package `quant/src/quant_edge/survival/`:

- **Censored-subject contracts.** `SubjectV1` (subject, group, stratum, duration,
  `EVENT`-with-cause or `RIGHT_CENSORED`, per-subject `QualityState`), bounded
  `CauseSetV1`, bounded strictly-increasing `TimeGridV1` with a right-closed binning
  law, and `SurvivalCohortV1` with posture `SYNTHETIC`/`REPLAY` only. A quality-gated
  cohort builder excludes ineligible subjects into measured `DATA_INVALID`-style
  exclusion counts (§11.7: gapped evidence is never a normal negative; censored cases
  remain censored).
- **Kaplan-Meier / Nelson-Aalen (§14.4).** Product-limit survival and cumulative
  hazard over exact integer risk-set/event counts, computed in exact rational
  arithmetic and quantized half-even to fixed point only at the contract boundary;
  events precede censorings at tied times (documented law); step evaluation returns
  `None` beyond observed follow-up (unknowns stay `UNKNOWN`). The reverse-KM
  censoring-survival estimator reuses the same machinery for IPCW.
- **Aalen-Johansen competing risks (§14.4).** Cause-specific cumulative incidence
  over the same exact counts; overall survival plus the sum of all cause incidences
  equals one exactly in the internal rationals at every step (a conservation law
  proved by property tests); single-cause AJ equals `1 - KM` exactly.
- **Stratified discrete-time cause-specific hazard model (§14.1/§14.4).** The
  transparent baseline survival model: per-stratum, per-bin, per-cause integer event
  and risk-set counts are the complete fitted state; a documented exact Laplace
  smoothing law; predictions (survival curve, cause CIFs, event probability at a
  horizon) are computed exactly from those counts and quantized at the boundary;
  unseen strata and risk sets below a declared minimum yield explicit `UNKNOWN`,
  never fabricated hazards; durations beyond the grid are administratively censored
  at grid end and counted. With a grid aligned to event times and no interior
  censoring the model's survival curve equals KM exactly (life-table equivalence,
  property-tested).
- **Time-dependent metrics (§14.4/§16.4).** A typed harness measuring: Brier score
  `BS(t)` on the grid with IPCW reverse-KM weights (weights fail closed to an
  explicit `UNAVAILABLE` report when the censoring survival hits zero, never a
  division by zero); integrated Brier score under an exact step-integration law;
  concordance (Harrell tie law with half credit, optional horizon truncation) with
  same-group pairs excluded and counted (§11.7 adjacent-window law); per-horizon
  calibration reports whose observed frequencies are censoring-aware (KM/AJ within
  each predicted-probability bin) with expected calibration error; a declared budget
  contract turning measurements into pass/fail truth.
- **Target vocabulary (§14.4).** Versioned v1 specs binding each of the six declared
  targets to its primary and competing causes (e.g. convergence competes with
  invalidation and disorder; §11.4 right-censored non-convergence). The vocabulary is
  machinery constants, not market truth.
- **Deterministic campaign.** `python -m quant_edge.survival.campaign OUTPUT_ROOT`
  writes, without network/env/ambient-time access:
  `quant/generated/q11/survival_golden_v1.json` (hand-verified golden curves, model
  predictions and metric reports), `quant/generated/q11/survival_campaign.v1.json`
  and `.md` (seed `20260712` seeded-cohort measurements: estimator recovery against
  known generating hazards, held-out calibration, IBS versus a marginal no-skill
  predictor, concordance, censoring rates, conservation and exclusion accounting);
  byte-identical across processes.
