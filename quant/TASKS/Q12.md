## Code and evidence artifacts

New package `quant/src/quant_edge/ensemble/`:

- **Fold-bound OOF contracts and stacking.** Consume Q14 `SampleSetV1`,
  `PredictionSetV1` and `WalkForwardPlanV1`. Every specialist bundle is bound to one
  valid fold's exact train and validation IDs. The union of OOF rows equals the union
  of valid fold validation rows exactly once; holdout/quarantine/train rows cannot
  enter meta-fitting. Target, horizon, probability semantics and feature-as-of lineage
  must match. A deterministic bounded convex stacker is fitted only from OOF rows and
  emits specialist outputs, weights, conflicts, missing specialists and digests.
- **Chronological calibration.** Compare bounded Platt/logistic and isotonic
  challengers on a calibration slice strictly later than stack fitting; insufficient,
  single-class, constant or non-chronological support yields `UNKNOWN`. Reports carry
  Brier, log loss, ECE at multiple predeclared bin counts, intercept/slope and
  reliability cells. Only a valid report grants calibrated-probability semantics.
- **Conformal intervals.** Exact split/conformalized-quantile baseline over ordered
  calibration residuals with a documented finite-sample order statistic; a bounded
  adaptive challenger updates only after an outcome becomes observable. Crossing,
  unavailable or under-supported intervals fail closed. Time-series results are
  labeled empirical/non-exchangeable; no unconditional coverage guarantee is claimed.
- **Uncertainty vector.** Keep source/data quality, Q09 OOD/disorder state, ensemble
  disagreement proxy, empirical outcome dispersion proxy, conformal interval width,
  cost/fill and timing/decay uncertainty separate. Never fabricate a scalar certainty,
  aleatoric variance or epistemic probability.
- **Abstention.** Deterministic ordered gates consume existing quality/disorder truth,
  specialist completeness/conflict, calibration/coverage, Q14 cost stress and bounded
  uncertainty. Stale/ineligible quality, OOD/unknown disorder, conflict, cost unknown,
  known cost-negative, stress instability, invalid calibration/interval or collapsed
  coverage yields final `ABSTAIN_UNKNOWN` with sorted stable reasons and absent
  probability/interval. Healthy agreeing positive-net evidence may yield only the
  frozen advisory action `WATCH`; Q12 conveys no strategy, capital, risk or order state.
- **Q10→Q14 closure manifest.** New digest-bound records consume the exact immutable
  Q10 bullet IDs below and name the Q14 owner APIs/evidence. Missing, duplicated or
  rewritten source dispositions fail closed:
  - `16-01-purged-embargoed-walk-forward` → `build_walk_forward_plan`
  - `16-02-untouched-holdout` → `HoldoutGrantV1` / `open_holdout`
  - `16-03-negative-control-factory` → Q14 controls / leakage assessment
  - `16-04-cost-latency-stress` → `run_cost_stress` plus Q12 abstention translation
  - `16-05-pbo-dsr-bootstrap` → Q14 trial ledger / PBO / DSR / bootstrap
