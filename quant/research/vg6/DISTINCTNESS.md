# V-G6 neighboring-lane distinction

This is a structural distinction fixed before outcomes, not a post-result
label.

| Lane | Event family | Mechanically excluded from V-G6 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V-G1 | BTC-only taker-buy pressure plus small price displacement | No taker-buy field, BTC specialization, or pressure threshold |
| V-G2 | Cross-sectional positive high-volume shock continuation | No volume-shock rank, positive-return rank, or cross-sectional top fraction |
| V-G3 | Negative residual plus high-volume shock mean reversion | No negative residual, delayed reversal entry, or contrarian direction |
| V-G4 | Prior drawdown/pivot followed by low-volume reversal | No drawdown, pivot, false-breakdown, reclaim, or reversal setup |
| V-G5 | Abnormal breakout-volume continuation at 1.5 or 2.0 times the prior 20-day median | Breakout-day quote volume must be strictly below 1.5 times that identical prior 20-day median denominator and is not a trial axis |

V-G6 alone requires both of these lagged pre-breakout conditions:

1. the prior 5- or 10-day high-low box is no wider than a frozen 4% or 7%; and
2. mean quote volume inside that box is no more than 50% or 75% of the
   non-overlapping preceding 60-day median.

It then requires an upper close breakout of the prior 20 completed days,
enters at the next UTC daily open, and holds for exactly 3 or 7 days.
